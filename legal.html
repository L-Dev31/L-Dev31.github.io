Privacy

Data controller: Léo Tosku, contact@leotosku.com.

What the portfolio collects. There is no account, no form, no advertising and no tracking across sites. Nothing is stored in your browser.

Audience measurement. To learn which parts of the portfolio are useful, the site counts anonymous events: pages viewed, links clicked, sections reached, how far a page is scrolled and for how long, the website you came from, the campaign tag of a link, if any, your country, device type, browser, operating system and language. No IP address is stored, and there is no cookie, no persistent identifier and no fingerprinting, so a visit cannot be linked to a person or to another visit. The measurement is designed to fit the CNIL exemption for strictly anonymous audience measurement. It is skipped when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR article 6.1.f). Events are kept for three months.

Technical logs. GitHub and Cloudflare process connection data, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site, under their own policies: GitHub and Cloudflare.

Third-party content. A video, where the site shows one, is loaded from YouTube only after you press play. Links to other sites, such as social networks, open those sites, which apply their own rules.

Email. If you write to contact@leotosku.com, your address and message are used only to reply. They are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox hosted by Google, and kept as long as the exchange needs.

Transfers outside the EU. GitHub, Cloudflare and Google are US companies. They rely on the transfer mechanisms described in their own policies.

Your rights. You can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to its processing (GDPR articles 15 to 21), by writing to contact@leotosku.com. You can also complain to the CNIL: cnil.fr.

Accessibility statement

I aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA and the French RGAA. Status: not audited, partly tested. This site has not had a full RGAA audit and has not been tested with screen readers. Automated checks on the main pages report no remaining WCAG A or AA violation that they can detect, which is not a claim of conformity.

Known limits:

  • Some brand colour pairs are below the recommended contrast ratio, for example white text on the red accent and the accent on light backgrounds in some project palettes.
  • The pixel portrait and text effects keep moving and have no pause control. They are reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • The page transitions and the custom cursor are decorative.

The site uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. To report a problem or ask for another format, write to contact@leotosku.com. If the answer is not satisfactory, you can contact the Défenseur des droits (formulaire.defenseurdesdroits.fr).
