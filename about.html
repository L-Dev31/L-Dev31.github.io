About me

I take a project from the first
sketch to the deployed site
and stay on it all the way.

I started young, making simple websites and minigames. Then I got hooked on taking games apart and rebuilding them, and game modding taught me English, 3D modelling, design, communication, team management and code. I grew up in Provence, moved to Guadeloupe and launched my first marketplace at 17. I now study on the French Riviera and bring that same deep work to companies. I like to work on a project as a whole: the research, the visual identity, the interface and the code. Doing it all myself lets me adapt the design to what the code allows, and the other way around, which is how a flag, a binary editor and a Creole translator ended up in the same portfolio.
