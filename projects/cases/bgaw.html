The Brief

As a group project at Sophia Ynov Campus, I led a four-person team auditing the digital strategy of BLACK GOOSE, an independent watchmaker based in Nice that blends automotive aesthetics with precision horology. The goal was an honest audit and a clear direction to raise their digital presence, with no full teardown.

I coordinated the project and handled the web concept and the logo redesign. Each section below credits the team member who owned that part.

Léo Tosku (web concept, logo) · Clément Dezon‑Hulin (art direction) · Benoîte Macchi (logo concept) · Timéo Renard (market research) · Ryan Aversa (photography)

Linking automotive design and watchmaking through a focused website that is easy to use.

The Problem

Premium Product, Weak Presence

BLACK GOOSE's watches deserve the same attention to detail as the automotive world that inspires them, but the digital presence did not show it. The site was cluttered and did not guide the user. Against established competitors like BRM, Depancel and Baltic, the brand needed a sharper identity and a smoother online journey.

The Process

1. Audit & Strategic Repositioning (Timéo Renard)

Timéo led the competitive benchmark, placing BLACK GOOSE among other luxury lifestyle brands. The audit showed a gap between a strong product and a weak structural language, so we aligned the new objectives on luxury automotive codes: minimal, confident, product-first.

2. Art Direction & Identity (Clément Dezon‑Hulin, Benoîte Macchi, Ryan Aversa)

Clément defined a dark, restrained visual direction that lets the watches stand out without competing decoration. Benoîte created the first logo concept, and Ryan shot original photography to extend the look to social media.

3. Logo Finalization (Léo Tosku)

Building on Benoîte's concept, I redesigned and finalized the mark in Illustrator. I tightened the geometry and corrected the proportions to give the brand's new identity a solid base.

4. Web Concept & User Journey (Léo Tosku)

I designed a minimal web interface focused on reducing friction. With secondary distractions removed, the layout guides visitors from discovery to purchase, in line with what a luxury audience expects.

Putting the product first meant removing distractions.

The Outcome

A cohesive brand and web concept, delivered by one team. The project showed the value of working within constraints and building on a partner's first idea without losing the core identity.
