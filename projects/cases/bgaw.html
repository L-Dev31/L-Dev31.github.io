The Brief

As part of a group project at Sophia Ynov Campus, I led a four-person team to audit and rethink the digital strategy for BLACK GOOSE, an independent Nice-based watchmaker blending automotive aesthetics with precision horlogery. The goal wasn't a full teardown, but an honest audit and a clear direction to elevate their digital presence.

I coordinated the project and directly handled the web concept and logo redesign. Each section below credits the specific team member who owned that part of the work, reflecting our shared direction.

Léo Tosku (web concept, logo) · Clément Dezon‑Hulin (art direction) · Benoîte Macchi (logo concept) · Timéo Renard (market research) · Ryan Aversa (photography)

Bridging automotive design and watchmaking through a focused, frictionless digital experience.

The Problem

Premium Product, Disconnected Presence

BLACK GOOSE’s watches demand the same attention to detail as the automotive industry that inspires them, but their digital presence didn't reflect this. The site was visually cluttered and failed to guide the user. Against established competitors like BRM, Depancel, and Baltic, BLACK GOOSE needed a sharper visual identity and a seamless online journey to stand out to a premium audience.

The Process

1. Audit & Strategic Repositioning (Timéo Renard)

Timéo led competitive benchmarking to map BLACK GOOSE against other luxury lifestyle brands. The audit revealed a clear gap between the strong product and its weak structural language, leading us to align the new business objectives around luxury automotive codes: minimal, confident, and product-first.

2. Art Direction & Identity (Clément Dezon‑Hulin, Benoîte Macchi, Ryan Aversa)

Clément defined a dark, restrained visual direction that lets the watches stand out without competing decoration. Benoîte created the initial logo concept, while Ryan shot original photography to elevate the brand's social media content and extend the aesthetic beyond the website.

3. Logo Finalization (Léo Tosku)

Building on Benoîte’s initial concept, I redesigned and finalized the mark in Illustrator. I tightened the geometry and corrected proportions to create a deliberate, premium logo that grounds the brand's new identity.

4. Web Concept & User Journey (Léo Tosku)

I designed a minimal, pixel-perfect web interface focused entirely on reducing user friction. By stripping away secondary distractions, the new layout guides users seamlessly from discovery to conversion, meeting the high expectations of a luxury audience.

Focusing on the product meant stripping away the noise.

The Outcome

A cohesive brand and web concept delivered by a unified team. This project proved the value of working within established constraints, building on a partner's initial vision, and executing a seamless digital experience without losing the core identity.
