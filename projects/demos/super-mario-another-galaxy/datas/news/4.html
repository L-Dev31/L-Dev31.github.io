The SMAG logo has been the visual identity of our project since its very beginning. Over the past five years, it has evolved alongside our development skills.


Here is how our logo evolved, from its first version in 2020 to the design we use today in 2025.


Each iteration shows artistic growth, technical improvement, and the team's dedication to the spirit of SMAG.


2020: The Beginning

It started in 2020 with our very first logo attempt. Like many first attempts, it was quite basic, but it carried the essence of what we wanted to achieve. Fun fact: the logo was made entirely in Pixellab on Android, for those who know! :p



2021: Refining the Vision

By 2021, Solargress had joined the team and brought a fresh perspective to our logo with his Photoshop skills at the time.



2022: Finding Our Style

The 2022 logo was the one that lasted the shortest time. It was actually L-Dev's first attempt at creating a 3D logo in Blender.



2023-2024: Professional Polish

From 2023 to 2024, our design skills had matured significantly. The logo received a major overhaul that brought it much closer to a traditional SMG logo style.



2025: The Current Logo

And here we are in 2025 with our current logo, the result of five years of design work. It was rendered in 3D through Blender and is heavily inspired by the new Super Mario Galaxy, the Movie.



The Design Philosophy Behind SMAG

Throughout this five-year evolution, several core principles have guided our logo design:

  • SMG Identity: Every version maintains a connection to SMG but must feel like a true glow up.
  • Readability: The logo must be clear and legible at any size
  • Professional Quality: Matching Nintendo's quality standards as close as possible.

Looking Forward

While we're proud of our current logo, we know design is never finished. As SMAG keeps growing, our visual identity might change too.


But for now, our 2025 logo shows five years of hard work, creativity and the team's dedication to making something special.
