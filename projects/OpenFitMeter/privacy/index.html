Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your privacy is our priority. Open Fit Meter is designed as a local-first application.

1. Data Collection

We collect location data (GPS), sensor data like accelerometer and barometer, and physical profile information such as height, weight, and gender to provide fitness tracking services.

2. Data Storage

Activity history, profile data and any imported rendering resource are stored locally on your device. There is no cloud storage or remote database, and activity history is excluded from Android cloud backup.

3. Data Sharing

Your activity data is never sold or shared with third parties. It leaves the device in only two cases: a peer-to-peer sync you start with your fitness console, and map tiles, which are downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers when a route map is shown (those requests reveal your IP address and the map area being viewed; no account data is sent with them).

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access and delete your data at any time. Use the "Clear History" or "Delete Account" options in the app settings to permanently erase your information.
